One ball. One platform. One more bounce.

Slide your finger to guide a glowing platform and keep the ball in the air. Find the sweet spot for a straight rebound or use the edges to change direction. Every successful bounce earns a point and raises the pace.

Stay focused as the platform begins to float up and down. How long can you keep your rhythm?

• Quick restarts and pause whenever you need
• Colorful minimalist visuals and optional sound
• Play offline — no ads, accounts, or purchases
