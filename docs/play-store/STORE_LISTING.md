# Google Play listing draft — review before submission

**Title:** Sky Bounce

**Short description:** Keep bouncing, master Reverse Mode, and unlock your own cosmic style.

**Full description:**

One ball. One platform. One more bounce.

Guide your platform with one finger and keep the ball alive. Find a steady rhythm, use the platform edges to steer, and react as the pace rises and the platform begins to float.

Choose your challenge:
• Classic Mode — catch the falling ball on a platform below.
• Reverse Mode — gravity pulls upward, so catch the ball at the top.

Make the game yours. Earn 15 free ball colors, paddle colors, and background themes through your best score. Equip each style individually, or keep the original appearance with its changing ball colors and drifting stars. Each mode saves its own best score.

The optional Sky Bounce Ultimate Style Pack adds 10 exclusive looks: Galaxy, Rainbow, Fireball and Chrome balls; Neon Pulse, Gold Rush and Cyber Grid paddles; Cyberpunk Skyline, Aurora Nights and Retro Arcade backgrounds. It is one permanent, non-consumable cosmetic purchase, with a planned US base price of $1.99. Local prices are shown by Google Play before payment. Paying does not change physics, scores, controls, or difficulty.

Play offline, restart quickly, pause whenever you need, and restore owned premium styles using your Google Play account when connected. No ads or subscriptions. The game itself does not require an account; optional purchases use Google Play.

**Suggested category:** Games → Arcade.

**In-app purchases disclosure:** Yes — one optional cosmetic pack. No randomized paid rewards, subscriptions, consumables, ads, or gameplay advantages.
